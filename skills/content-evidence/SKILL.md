# Content Evidence

1. Inventory source material and identify its provenance.
2. Classify each claim as verified, inferred, missing, contradictory, or obsolete.
3. Preserve uncertainty instead of replacing it with persuasive but unsupported copy.
4. Define one canonical location for facts that need reuse across routes, retrieval, or prompts.
5. Give Copy, Prompt Engineer, or Frontend a compact trusted fact set and explicit exclusions.

Raw assets and generated descriptions are not canonical facts until reviewed against their source.
