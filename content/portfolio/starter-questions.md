## Capability Shortcut Eligibility

Eligible canonical shortcuts:

- Internal tools and operational workflows.
- Product and workflow design.
- Design systems and reusable component logic.
- AI-assisted product workflows, with project-specific maturity limitations.
- Information architecture and complex decision surfaces.
- Front-end prototyping/implementation, described as an authored capability rather than a job title.

Technology tags alone do not create a capability. Each shortcut must resolve to at least one approved claim ID above.

## Company/Context Shortcut Eligibility

- `Litres` and `Wrike`: eligible as public organization/context labels.
- `Peruse`: eligible as a project/product label; `Peruse.ml` is its alias.
- `DS Hito`: eligible as a project/design-system label; organization status should not be expanded beyond the authored external link.
- `VK`, `CityMobile`, and `CityMobil`: omit as a single company shortcut until Product supplies the canonical relationship. They may still be search aliases that lead to the Taxi project with an uncertainty disclosure.
